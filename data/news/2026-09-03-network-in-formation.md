---
title: The network is forming
date: 2026-09-03
---

Computational and cognitive neuroscience labs across Hong Kong, Shenzhen,
Guangzhou and Macau are setting up a joint seminar series and a shared roster.
The site, the data behind it and the rules the network runs by are all in a
public repository. Labs in the region can [join now](../join/), and anyone can
[nominate a speaker](https://github.com/XBTinChina/Greater-bay-CCN-association/issues/new?template=speaker-nomination.yml)
for the first season.
