The [SMRT Lab](https://labsmrt.github.io/), in the Department of Psychology at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, is seeking a Postdoctoral Fellow to investigate sensorimotor coordination and the perception and production of rhythm, while developing AI-assisted workflows for scientific research.

Our research asks how the brain detects temporal patterns, anticipates upcoming events, and coordinates perception and action, particularly in speech and music. We combine behavioural experiments, neuroscience methods, and computational modelling to understand these processes. Learn more about our [research directions](https://labsmrt.github.io/research/).

This position brings together experimental and computational research with the practical development of AI-assisted research tools.

**The fellow will contribute to:**

- **Sensorimotor and rhythm research:** developing hypotheses and designing experiments on timing, rhythmic behaviour, and the relationship between sensory information and action.
- **Data analysis and computational modelling:** analysing behavioural and neural data and building models that connect experimental findings to explanations of perception and behaviour.
- **Research automation:** developing and evaluating workflows that use AI to support literature synthesis, hypothesis generation, experimental design, code development, analysis, and model testing.
- **Reproducible research:** building documented workflows with appropriate validation and quality checks, so that AI-assisted outputs can be examined and reproduced.

**We are looking for applicants with:**

- A PhD in neuroscience, psychology, computer science, cognitive science, engineering, or a related field.
- Strong programming and quantitative skills, with experience in scientific computing, statistical analysis, or computational modelling.
- An interest in sensorimotor coordination, rhythm, and the links between brain and behaviour.
- Enthusiasm for combining experimental research with AI-assisted automation.

Experience with behavioural experiments, neural data analysis, machine learning, or AI research tools would be particularly relevant.

**How to apply**

Email [XiangbinTeng@cuhk.edu.hk](mailto:XiangbinTeng@cuhk.edu.hk) with your CV and a brief statement of research interests, describing your relevant experience and how it connects to the position. You may also include links to publications, code repositories, or other research projects, together with your anticipated availability.
