Thank you for speaking in the GBA-CCN Network seminar series. Your host will confirm the date, time and format, send any access details and help with practical questions. If the details below differ from your invitation or event page, please check with your host.

## Who we are

The Greater Bay Area Computational and Cognitive Neuroscience Network (GBA-CCN Network, 粤港澳大湾区计算神经科学与认知神经科学网络) connects labs across the Greater Bay Area. Volunteer coordinators support the network, and member labs take turns hosting seminars. Your host lab arranges the session and provides a chair. The participating groups are listed on [our labs page](../../labs/).

## Who is in the audience

The audience includes undergraduates, postgraduate students, postdocs, research staff and faculty from several institutions and research fields. A short introduction to the question and methods will help people outside your area follow the talk.

English is the usual language for talks. Questions may be asked in English, Cantonese or Mandarin, with the chair helping to summarise in English when needed. Please tell your host about any language or accessibility needs in advance.

## Format and timing

The usual slot is the second Friday of the month at 16:00 Hong Kong Time (UTC+8). Individual events may use another date or time. The confirmed invitation and event page give the arrangements for your session.

The usual format is a 45-minute talk followed by 15 minutes of questions. Some sessions begin with an additional 15-minute early-career talk by a student or postdoc. For a session starting at 16:00, this means the main talk begins at about 16:15 and the session ends at 17:15. Without the opening talk, the session runs from 16:00 to 17:00. Your host will confirm which format applies.

## Platform and hybrid sessions

Seminars are online by default on Zoom, with Tencent Meeting (VooV) as a fallback. Your host will confirm which platform to use. A host lab may also arrange an in-person audience for a hybrid session.

## Time zones

Hong Kong Time is UTC+8 throughout the year, so 16:00 Hong Kong Time is 08:00 UTC. Your local time may vary with daylight-saving changes. Ask your host to confirm your local date and time for the actual event date. If the time is difficult for you, contact your host early so that they can discuss options with the coordinators.

## What to send your host

Please send the following about four weeks before the talk so that the host can prepare the event page and announcement:

- a title and an abstract of one or two paragraphs;
- a short biography of three or four sentences;
- a photo for the announcement, if you would like to provide one;
- your decision about recording the talk;
- your decision about publishing slides afterwards;
- any accessibility needs, such as captions, a break or a particular room setup.

## Recording and publication

We record only with your written consent; an email to your host is enough. With your consent, recordings are posted on Bilibili and YouTube and linked from the event page. You may decline recording or withdraw consent later by contacting your host. Slides are published only with your permission.

## Honorarium and travel

The network has no budget of its own and does not normally offer an honorarium. A host institution may fund travel for an in-person visit. Any support must be confirmed in the invitation; please check with your host before making travel arrangements.

## Technical checklist

- Use a stable internet connection; a wired connection can help when available.
- Use a headset or earphones with a microphone to reduce echo.
- Share the slide window so that notifications and other windows stay private.
- Join ten minutes early to check audio, video and screen sharing.
- Tell your host in advance if you will play audio or video so that sound sharing can be tested.
- Allow the agreed time for questions. A host-lab member will monitor the chat and read out written questions.

## After the talk

If you agreed to recording, your host will send the links when they are available. The event page may also link to your slides and research page, with your permission. Contact your host if any information needs correcting or removing.

## Code of conduct

The network's [code of conduct](../code-of-conduct/) applies to speakers, chairs and attendees. Contact your host privately if you have a concern.
