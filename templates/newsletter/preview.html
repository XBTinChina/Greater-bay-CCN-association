Neural basis of aphantasia and conscious access to imagery

Jianghao Liu
Friday, 4 December 2026 · 16:00 Hong Kong Time (UTC+8)
Online via Zoom

How can people who cannot voluntarily form mental images still perform tasks involving visual imagery? Jianghao Liu will present findings from brain imaging and eye-tracking, and a model of how internal representations become conscious images.
